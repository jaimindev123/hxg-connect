Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

HXG Connect is a customer relationship system for real estate developers, builders, agencies and brokers. This policy explains what personal data the service handles, why, and what choices people have. It is written to meet the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

1. Who is responsible for what

Two kinds of data pass through HXG Connect, and responsibility differs.

DataWho decides how it is usedOur role
Client data: enquiries, buyers, calls, messages, bookings and staff check-ins that a company (our "client") keeps in its workspaceThe client. They are the data fiduciary.Data processor. We handle it only on the client's instructions, to run the service.

If you enquired about a property and want to know what a builder or broker holds about you, ask that company. We will help them respond.

2. What is collected

  • Buyers and enquirers: name, phone numbers, email, the property you asked about, budget, preferred location, where the enquiry came from (for example a website form, a property portal or an advertisement), and the history of contact with you: notes, follow-ups, site visits, bookings and payments.
  • Calls: when a client connects a telephony provider, the time, duration and outcome of calls. Calls are recorded only if the client has switched recording on. The client is responsible for telling callers that a call may be recorded.
  • WhatsApp messages exchanged with the client's business number, when the client connects WhatsApp. Replying STOP opts you out.
  • Client staff: name, work email, role, and activity in the system. Sales staff who use field check-in share a selfie taken at that moment and the device's location at that moment. Location is never tracked in the background, and photos cannot be picked from the gallery.
  • Channel partners: name, firm, contact details, the buyers they register and their commission records.
  • Technical records: IP address and browser type in security logs, and a record of sign-ins and significant actions.

We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics inside the product. The only browser storage used is what keeps you signed in and remembers light or dark mode.

3. Why it is used

  • To let the client respond to enquiries, follow up, arrange visits, record bookings and collect payments.
  • To let the client's managers supervise their sales team, including verifying site visits.
  • To keep accounts secure, prevent misuse, and investigate problems.
  • To bill our clients and meet tax and accounting law.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use client data to market to buyers or to train AI models.

4. Who it is shared with

  • Each client sees only its own workspace. Workspaces are separated at the database level.
  • Services the client connects: its telephony provider, WhatsApp Business (Meta), Razorpay for payments, and its email provider. Data goes to these only for the function the client switched on.
  • Our infrastructure providers: the hosting company where the service runs and the email service that sends invitations and password resets.
  • Our support staff can open a client's workspace only after the client's owner approves a time-limited support session. Every such session is logged and visible to the client.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

5. How long it is kept

Client data stays for as long as the client keeps its account. When a client closes its account, we delete its workspace or return an export on written request, within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep, such as invoices. Backups roll off within 30 days after that. Security logs are kept for up to one year.

6. Security

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored hashed. Integration keys are encrypted at rest.
  • Each client's data is isolated by row-level security in the database.
  • Access is by role inside each workspace: salespeople see their own leads, team leaders their team's.
  • Selfies and call recordings are served through signed, expiring links or permission-checked requests, never public addresses.
  • Our own staff console supports two-step sign-in.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data we will notify the affected clients and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask for a summary of your personal data, have it corrected or erased, withdraw consent, nominate someone to act for you, and raise a grievance.

  • For data a builder, agency or broker holds about you, contact that company. They control it.
  • For your HXG Connect account, write to info@hxgglobal.com. We reply within 7 working days.

If you are not satisfied with a response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Children

HXG Connect is a business tool and is not meant for anyone under 18.

9. Where data is stored

Data is stored on servers in the region our hosting is set up in. If data is transferred outside India, it is done in line with the DPDP Act.

10. Changes

We will post changes here and change the date at the top. For significant changes we will tell client account owners by email.
